Namu voice pack "INT8, сжатые веса" (int8w)

This pack contains a modified version (a "Derivative of the Model") of Supertonic 3 by Supertone Inc.,
https://huggingface.co/Supertone/supertonic-3 (commit 3cadd1ee6394adea1bd021217a0e650ede09a323),
licensed under the BigScience OpenRAIL-M License (see LICENSE).

Changes made by the Namu project:
- 2026-11-17 (version 1): onnx/duration_predictor.onnx, onnx/text_encoder.onnx,
  onnx/vector_estimator.onnx, onnx/vocoder.onnx: weights of Conv/MatMul/Gemm layers are stored as INT8
  (symmetric, per output channel) and expanded back to float32 by DequantizeLinear nodes when the
  model is loaded.
- 2026-11-20 (version 2): onnx/vocoder.onnx: weights are stored as FP16 instead of INT8 (expanded by
  Cast nodes), because INT8 vocoder weights added audible hiss (noise 19 dB below the speech; with
  FP16 it is 56 dB below). The other files are unchanged from version 1.
The network architecture and the voice style files are unchanged.
